About Our Leadership

About Our CEO
Mian Ejaz Aslam

Mian Ejaz Aslam is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of REI GOLDEN STAR TRAVEL & TOURS (PVT) LTD. A visionary entrepreneur with years of experience in the travel and tourism industry, he has dedicated his career to providing reliable, transparent, and customer-focused travel solutions.

With expertise in Umrah & Hajj services, international visit visas, air ticketing, travel consultancy, and corporate travel management, he has built the company on the principles of trust, integrity, professionalism, and service excellence. His leadership has enabled REI GOLDEN STAR TRAVEL & TOURS to establish long-term relationships with clients and business partners by consistently delivering high-quality travel experiences.

Believing that every journey should be comfortable, secure, and memorable, Mian Ejaz Aslam continues to drive innovation and uphold international service standards. His vision is to make REI GOLDEN STAR TRAVEL & TOURS (PVT) LTD. one of Pakistan's most trusted and respected travel companies, offering world-class travel solutions with complete customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

REI GOLDEN STAR TRAVEL & TOURS (PVT) LTD

At REI GOLDEN STAR TRAVEL & TOURS (PVT) LTD, our vision is to become one of Pakistan's most trusted and internationally recognized travel companies by delivering exceptional travel experiences with professionalism, integrity, and innovation.

We aspire to redefine the travel industry by providing reliable, affordable, and customer-centric solutions that meet the evolving needs of modern travelers. Through continuous improvement, advanced technology, and strategic global partnerships, we aim to simplify travel while maintaining the highest standards of quality and service.

Our vision extends beyond booking flights and arranging tours—we strive to build lasting relationships with our clients by earning their trust through transparency, excellence, and personalized support. Whether it is Umrah & Hajj services, international visit visas, air ticketing, holiday packages, or corporate travel solutions, our goal is to make every journey smooth, secure, and memorable.

As we continue to grow, we remain committed to expanding our global network, embracing innovation, and setting new benchmarks in the travel industry. Our ambition is to be the first choice for individuals, families, and businesses seeking dependable travel services while contributing to the growth of Pakistan's tourism and travel sector.
