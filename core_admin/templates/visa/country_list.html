How to Avoid Visa Rejections

Embassies demand clear financial and personal ties to Pakistan. If a single document is inconsistent, it can lead to visa refusal. Follow these expert recommendations to secure approval.

Clear Source of Bank Deposits

Large sudden cash deposits without supporting documentation (e.g. sale deed, salary slip) raise red flags. Maintain steady transaction flows.

Precise Travel Itinerary

Your flight reserves, hotel bookings, and day-by-day travel plan must align perfectly. We provide verified reservations for this purpose.

Common Refusal Factors

1. Insufficient Proof of Ties

Failure to submit property ownership documents, active employment letters, or immediate family certificates.

2. Incomplete Financial Records

Filing bank statements without bank maintenance letters signed and stamped by the branch manager.
