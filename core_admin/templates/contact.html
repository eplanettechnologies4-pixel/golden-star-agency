Support FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about our platform and response times.

What is the average response time for support queries?

We aim to answer all direct messages and emails within 2 to 4 hours during operational timing (9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday). Emergency ticketing queries are processed 24/7.

How can travel agents verify their bank vouchers?

Agents can upload digital copies of bank deposit vouchers directly through the dashboard interface under the "Billing" section. Our financial department validates vouchers within 30 minutes.

Do you offer API support for external developers?

Yes, we provide custom JSON API integrations for partner agency systems to search flight schedules and ticket prices. Please drop an inquiry email to our ticketing desk for keys.
