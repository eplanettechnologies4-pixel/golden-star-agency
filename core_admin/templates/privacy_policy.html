Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026. How we protect agent databases and traveler parameters.

1. Scope and Introduction

Golden Star Agency ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Golden Star travel platform. We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding the privacy of our partner travel agents, pilgrim candidates, flight passengers, and portal visitors in Pakistan. This policy governs how we collect, store, and share your personal files.

2. Information We Collect

To process bookings, issue visa sticker advisories, and maintain agent wallets, we collect the following datasets:

  • Agent Profile Data: Full name, CNIC number, business coordinates, bank deposit voucher details, and wallet balance receipts.
  • Traveler Parameters: Scanned copies of passports, bio registration states, vaccination alerts, and flight seat preferences.
  • Usage Records: IP addresses, browser specs, booking logs, and transaction timestamps.

3. How We Use Collected Data

We process information strictly for valid booking objectives:

  • To verify B2B agent wallet credits and process deposits.
  • To file electronic visa applications at destination embassies.
  • To book GDS ticketing codes and lock hotel reservations in Makkah and Madinah.
  • To analyze chart trend parameters on the admin dashboard.

4. Security Measures

All credit ledgers and passport copies are encrypted using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) channels. We employ network firewalls and database access policies to protect against unverified viewing of agent data.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We only share traveler parameters with certified airline companies, tour operators, and Saudi Hajj/Umrah systems when necessary to issue tickets or Nusuk app permits. We do not sell or lease agent contact listings to third-party advertising companies.

6. Support and Inquiries

If you have queries regarding details removal or database corrections, please trigger a message to our support line at support@goldenstaragency.com.
