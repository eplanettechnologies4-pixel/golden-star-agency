How to Perform Umrah: Essential Steps

For first-time pilgrims, here is a concise visual summary of the four pillars of Umrah.

Step 1

Ihram & Niyyah

Enter the state of purity (Ihram) at the designated Miqat boundary. Recite Talbiyah and set your intention for the pilgrimage.

Step 2

Tawaf of the Kaaba

Perform seven counter-clockwise circuits around the Holy Kaaba starting from the Black Stone (Hajar al-Aswad), reciting supplications.

Step 3

Sa'ee (Safa & Marwa)

Walk seven times between the historic hills of Safa and Marwa in remembrance of Hajar (A.S)'s search for water for Prophet Ismail (A.S).

Step 4

Halq or Taqseer

Shave (Halq) or shorten (Taqseer) your hair. This releases you from the rules of Ihram, concluding your Umrah rites successfully.

Umrah Medical & E-Visa Regulations

Nusuk App Permit Booking

All pilgrims must book Umrah slots and Rawdah prayer permits via the Nusuk Application. Walk-in entry is strictly prohibited by security officers.

Meningitis Vaccine: Compulsory 10 days before flight.

Biometrics: Mandatory via Saudi Bio mobile application.

Validity: Passport must be valid for at least 6 months.
