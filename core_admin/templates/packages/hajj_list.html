Day-by-Day Hajj Pilgrimage Timeline

Understanding the essential phases of your sacred journey helps you prepare mentally and physically.

Day 1

Ihram & Mina

Pilgrims wear Ihram, declare Niyyah, and travel to the tent city of Mina on the 8th of Dhul Hijjah, spending the day and night in prayer.

Day 2

Arafah & Muzdalifah

The pinnacle of Hajj on the 9th. Pilgrims stand in Arafat, then travel to Muzdalifah at sunset to collect pebbles and sleep under the open sky.

Day 3

Ramy & Tawaf

On the 10th (Eid), pilgrims perform Jamarat stoning, sacrifice animals, shave/trim hair (Halq), and travel to Makkah for Tawaf al-Ifadah.

Days 4-5

Tashreeq & Farewell

Return to Mina for 2-3 days of stoning the Jamarat, concluding the Hajj with Tawaf al-Wida (Farewell Tawaf) in Makkah before returning home.

Mandatory Requirements & Prep Checklist

A successful pilgrimage depends heavily on early preparation. The Saudi Ministry of Hajj enforces strict medical and documentary protocols for all Pakistani nationals.

Vaccination Cards & PCR

Valid Meningococcal Meningitis (ACYW-135), Influenza, and Polio vaccination certificates are required.

Saudi Bio App Registration

All Pakistani passport holders must enroll biometric details via the official app before visa issuance.

Physical Endurance Prep

We advise pilgrims to walk 4-5 kilometers daily for at least 4 weeks before their flight departure date.

Hajj FAQs & Guidelines

What is the difference between Shifting & Non-Shifting?

Shifting packages move you to building apartments away from the Haram during peak Hajj days (4-12 Dhul Hijjah) to save costs. Non-Shifting packages guarantee hotel stays next to the Haram for the entire trip duration.

What is included in the Maktab categories (A, B, C)?

Maktab categories define your proximity to the Jamarat (stoning area) inside Mina, tent capacity, mattress types, and catering options. Category A is closest with gypsum-board private tents, while Category C is further away with basic carpets.
